# Changelog

What's new in each TestFlight build of WhatTheFlight, newest first. Notes for the next build collect in `ReleaseNotes/unreleased/` and are folded in here by `/release-notes` when the build goes out.

## 1.0 (11) — 2026-10-28

### New
- **What it's doing** — under the aircraft's type, the radar's center says what it's up to: "On approach to ATL · 12 mi out", "Climbing out of ATL", "Cruising · 2h 05m to DFW". It's on the details too, and Apple Watch shows the short form ("Approach ATL").
- **Favorite aircraft** — tap Favorite on an aircraft's details (or swipe it in the Aircraft list, or use its logbook page) and it's yellow wherever it turns up: its icon on the radar and the map, a heart by its flight number, its name in your logbook and stats. Favorites lead the Aircraft list, with Where Is It Now? for any not nearby, stay yellow whatever flight the airframe is on, and sync to iPad, Apple TV and Apple Watch.
- **Can I see it?** — the radar's center says when an aircraft can't be seen and why: above the clouds, lost in haze, too far to make out, low on the horizon or near the sun. Prefer aircraft you can see puts the nearest visible one in the center. Both in Settings → Radar → Weather. *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Clouds and fog** — drift across the radar and the map with the wind, from Apple Weather, or the local airport reports when it isn't available. Settings → Radar → Weather → Clouds and fog. *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Watchlist alerts** — get an alert when a tail number, type, airline or callsign comes into range (Settings → Alerts → Watchlist, or Alert Me on any aircraft's details). Also new: alerts when a favorite or pinned aircraft comes back into range, and for the first of a new type or airline in your logbook. Apple Watch taps your wrist for them.
- **Altitude vs speed** — the logbook's Stats tab opens on every aircraft you've logged as a dot at its closest pass, colored by airline, cargo, business jet, general aviation, helicopter or military. Tap a dot, or drag along them, to read one. *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Route web** — on Stats, your sky's busiest route airports round a ring, with a ribbon for each route from origin to destination. Tap an airport or a ribbon for its routes, then open those flights. *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Explore the sky charts** — tap a petal on Which way they fly, or an hour or weekday under When your sky is busiest (or drag along the bars), for its share of your sky, how it ranks, and the airlines, types and routes behind it. "See the … aircraft" lists every one. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Stats charts full screen** — Stats opens with Which way they fly beside the route web and altitude vs speed underneath, and a click opens any chart full screen, the Overview's last 14 days included. The remote's arrows step through directions, hours, days, altitudes, airports or dots, the panel beside the chart says who flies it, and a click lists those aircraft. *(Apple TV)*
- **Your month and year in the sky** — a recap on the logbook's Overview: six cards led by charts (a calendar of your busiest days, a 24-hour clock and which way they flew, the mix of airlines, cargo, private and military, your top airlines, height and speed with your records ringed, badges as medals). Share them as pictures from iPhone and iPad; on Apple TV they're widescreen and ◀ ▶ flip through them. *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Follow on Lock Screen** — from an aircraft's details: its route, a progress bar and an arrival countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, wherever it flies, until it lands. *(iPhone)*
- **Next Overhead widget** — for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy: a countdown to the next aircraft passing over you and which way to look; the medium size has a Start Sky Watch button. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Sky View flight path** — a dashed line where the aircraft you're aiming at will fly over the next ten minutes (purple by day, yellow after sunset), plus "Rising soon" for aircraft still below the trees. *(iPhone)*
- **Aircraft details** — the maker and exact model ("Boeing 717-231") and the company or organization it's registered to, never a private owner. Settings → Display → Aircraft details. *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Metric units** — Settings → Radar → Units switches every distance, altitude and speed to kilometers, meters and km/h: radar, logbook, badges, spoken alerts, widgets and Apple Watch. New installs follow your region.
- **Backup data source** — if Flightradar24 stops answering, the radar switches to adsb.lol's open data on its own ("Backup data · adsb.lol" along the bottom; no routes) and back when Flightradar24 returns. Settings → Radar → Data source can pin one.
- **Saved spots** — name the places you watch from in Settings → Location and switch between them with a tap; the Apple TV has the same list, and a spot sent from your iPhone keeps its name. *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Share card** — Share on a flight's details, or on a logbook sighting, sends a picture with its route, type, altitude, speed and closest pass over a map of where it flew. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Add from Photos** — file your own camera-roll shots under a logbook sighting, with the boarding pass to share; they show in the Photos tab and sync like Sky View photos. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Export & backup** — Settings → Logbook saves a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full backup with every sighting and photo to Files; Restore from Backup merges one back without duplicates. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Ask Siri about your logbook** — "Have I seen this plane before?" and "How many planes have I seen today?"; every aircraft in your logbook also turns up in Spotlight. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Evening digest** — an optional notification (8 pm, or a time you pick) summing up the day's sky. Settings → Logbook. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Read alerts aloud** — Settings → Alerts → Read alerts aloud; with Sky Watch on it works with the phone locked, by default only through AirPods, headphones or CarPlay. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Fourteen new app icons** — in Settings → Display → App Icon, with an Automatic / Light / Dark switch to keep an icon light or dark whatever your Home Screen is set to. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Apple Watch logbook** — aircraft the Watch sees on its own join your logbook through iCloud; an aircraft's details say when you've seen it before, settings show today's count, and earning a badge taps your wrist. *(Apple Watch)*
- **Battery saver** — the Watch radar updates less often, without the sweep or animations; Identify and Find work as usual. *(Apple Watch)*

### Improved
- **Radar bar** — matches the logbook's Liquid Glass tab bar: wider, with bigger purple icons, clearer labels and a soft glow under your finger. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Aircraft list** — no pin button on every row: pick an aircraft to bring it to the radar and pin it from its details. Unpin one that isn't flying with a swipe (iPhone, iPad) or by holding select (Apple TV). *(iPhone, iPad, Apple TV)*
- **Flight details** — the cities under the airport codes are spelled out in full, big enough to read. *(iPhone, iPad)*
- **Apple Watch alert settings** — change how close counts as overhead and how far ahead the wrist tap comes, and switch favorite and pin alerts on or off. *(Apple Watch)*
