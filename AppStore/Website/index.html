What's that plane?

What The Flight? shows every aircraft in the sky around you, live, on a radar that turns as you turn. Tap the nearest for its flight, route and photo, or raise your iPhone to see them labeled in the sky.

RadarThe nearest aircraft in the center, the rest on the edge in their real direction.
Sky ViewPoint your iPhone up and the camera labels the planes you can see.
LogbookEvery plane that passes is logged, with records and badges to earn.
AlertsA heads-up when something passes overhead or something rare flies by.
Apple WatchPoint your arm at a plane to find out what it is.
Apple TVThe sky on the big screen: radar, map, or both.
