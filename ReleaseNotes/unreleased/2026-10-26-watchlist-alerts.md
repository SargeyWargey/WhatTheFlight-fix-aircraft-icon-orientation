Watchlist alerts: tell the app about a tail number, an aircraft type, an airline or a callsign (Settings → Alerts → Watchlist, or Alert Me on any aircraft's details) and you'll get an alert whenever one comes into range. There are also new alerts when a favorite or pinned aircraft comes back into range, and for the first of a new type or airline in your logbook. Apple Watch taps your wrist for them too.
Try: open an airliner's details, choose Alert Me → Any of its type, and wait for the next one.
