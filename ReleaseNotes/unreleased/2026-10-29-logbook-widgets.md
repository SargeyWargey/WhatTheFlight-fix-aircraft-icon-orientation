Three new widgets for your logbook. Logbook shows today's count, your streak, the last few weeks as bars, and your records. Badges fills a badge's ring toward its next level; on iPad's extra-large size it shows every badge as a trophy case. Your Sky is a 24-hour clock of when aircraft pass over you, with a rose of which way they fly. They come in Home Screen and Lock Screen sizes.
Try: add the Badges widget, press and hold it, choose Edit Widget and pick a badge to follow. Tap a badge to open its page.
