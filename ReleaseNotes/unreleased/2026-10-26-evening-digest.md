Evening digest: turn it on in Settings → Logbook and, on days you logged something, a notification at 8 pm (or the time you pick) sums up your sky: how many aircraft, new types and airlines, and the closest pass. Tap it to open the logbook.
Try: turn on Evening digest, set the time a few minutes ahead, and leave the app after logging a few aircraft.
