Apple Watch keeps the logbook too: aircraft it sees on its own (not when watching your iPhone's spot) go into the same logbook as your iPhone's through iCloud. An aircraft's details say when you've seen it before, the settings page shows today's count, and earning a badge taps your wrist and shows it.
Try: leave your iPhone behind, watch an aircraft pass with only your Watch, then find it in the iPhone's logbook logged on Apple Watch.
