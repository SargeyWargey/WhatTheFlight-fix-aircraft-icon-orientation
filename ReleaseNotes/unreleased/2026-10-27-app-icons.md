Fourteen new app icons in Settings → Display → App Icon: eight playful takes on "What the…?!" (Interrobang, Cloud Mark, Wide-Eyed, Loop-the-Loop and more) and six about planes and your logbook stats (Tally, Sky by the Hour, Badge Ring and more). A new Automatic / Light / Dark switch at the top keeps an icon light or dark whatever your Home Screen is set to, or lets it follow along.
Try: pick Wide-Eyed, set the switch to Dark, then switch your iPhone to light mode and check the Home Screen.
