Sky View now draws a dashed line ahead of the aircraft you're aiming at, showing where it'll fly over the next ten minutes: purple by day, turning yellow after sunset so it stands out against the sky. It also lists aircraft still below the trees that are about to rise into view: "Rising soon: DL570 from the SW in 2:10".
Try: lift your phone toward an approaching aircraft and follow the dashed line, once by day and once after dark.
