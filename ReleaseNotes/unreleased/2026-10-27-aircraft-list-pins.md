The Aircraft list is tidier: no more pin button on every row. Pick an aircraft to bring it to the radar, and pin it from its details. A pinned aircraft that isn't flying can still be unpinned from the list: swipe it on iPhone and iPad, or hold the select button on Apple TV (a click shows where it is now).
Try: open Aircraft, pick a flight, then pin it from its details.
