The month and year recap cards now lead with charts. You'll see a calendar of your busiest days, a 24-hour clock of when aircraft came over and which way they flew, and coloured squares for the mix of airlines, cargo, private and military. There are also ranking bars for the airlines you saw most, every aircraft plotted by height and speed with your records ringed, and your badges as medals. On Apple TV the cards are widescreen, and the remote's left and right flip through them.
Try: open the logbook, tap "Your 2026", flip through the six cards (on Apple TV with ◀ ▶) and share one from iPhone.
