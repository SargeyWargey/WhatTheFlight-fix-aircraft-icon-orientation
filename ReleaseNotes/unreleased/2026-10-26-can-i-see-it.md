"Can I see it?" The radar's centre now says when the aircraft can't be seen and why: above the clouds, lost in haze, too far to make out, low on the horizon or close to the sun. It uses the nearest airport weather report and where the sun is, and says nothing when you can see it. Turn it off, or turn on Prefer aircraft you can see to put the nearest visible aircraft in the centre, in Settings → Radar → Weather.
Try: on a cloudy day, check the line under a high aircraft's type, then turn on Prefer aircraft you can see.
