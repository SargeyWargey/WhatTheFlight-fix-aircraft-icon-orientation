Metric units: Settings → Radar → Units switches every distance, altitude and speed to kilometers, meters and km/h, on the radar, in the logbook and badges, in spoken alerts, on widgets and on Apple Watch. New installs start in whatever your region uses.
Try: switch Units to Metric and check the radar's mile labels, the hero's altitude and a logbook record all change.
