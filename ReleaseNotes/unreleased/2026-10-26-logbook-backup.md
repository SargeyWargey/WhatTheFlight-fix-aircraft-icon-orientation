Export and back up your logbook: Settings → Logbook → Export & backup saves a spreadsheet (CSV) with a row per sighting, or a backup folder with every sighting and photo to Files. Restore from Backup merges one back in, on this device or a new one, without losing anything already there.
Try: back up your logbook to Files, then restore it and check nothing is duplicated.
