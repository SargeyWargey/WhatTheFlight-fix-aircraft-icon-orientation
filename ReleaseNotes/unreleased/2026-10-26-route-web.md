New on the logbook's Stats tab: a route web showing where the aircraft over your sky were going. The busiest route airports sit round a ring, with a ribbon for each route pointing from origin to destination. Tap an airport or a ribbon to see its routes, then open those flights. On Apple TV, select the web to open it full screen: left and right go round the airports, down and up through their routes, and a click shows the flights.
Try: tap your home airport and follow its busiest route to the flights.
