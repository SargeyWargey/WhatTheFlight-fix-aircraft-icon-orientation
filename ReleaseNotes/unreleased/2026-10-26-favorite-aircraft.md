Favorite aircraft: tap Favorite on an aircraft's details (or swipe it in the Aircraft list, or use the button on its logbook page) and it's called out in yellow wherever it turns up: its whole icon on the radar and the map, a heart by its flight number, and its name in your logbook and stats. Favorites sit at the top of the Aircraft list, with Where Is It Now? for any that aren't nearby, and follow you to your iPad, Apple TV and Apple Watch. A favorite is the airframe itself, so it stays yellow whatever flight it's on.
Try: open an aircraft's details, tap Favorite, then find it in yellow on the radar and at the top of the Aircraft list.
