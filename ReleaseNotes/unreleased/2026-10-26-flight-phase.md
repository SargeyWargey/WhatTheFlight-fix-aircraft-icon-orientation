The radar's centre now says what the aircraft is doing, under its type: "On approach to ATL · 12 mi out", "Climbing out of ATL", "Cruising · 2h 05m to DFW". It's on the details too, and Apple Watch shows the short form ("Approach ATL").
Try: watch an airliner near an airport and see the line change from cruising to approach.
