Ask Siri about your logbook: "Have I seen this plane before?" checks the aircraft nearest you (or a tail number you name), and "How many planes have I seen today?" sums up your day. Every aircraft in your logbook also turns up in Spotlight search, and tapping one opens its page.
Try: say "Have I seen this plane before in What The Flight", or search Spotlight for a tail number you've logged.
