Clouds and fog now drift across the radar and the map, moving with the wind: scattered clouds in patches, overcast as a blanket, fog as a milky haze. They come from Apple Weather around you (its mark sits above the Maps logo on the map), or from the local airport weather reports when Apple Weather isn't available, so it shows broadly where the weather is rather than a satellite picture. Turn it off in Settings → Radar → Weather → Clouds and fog.
Try: on a cloudy or foggy day, open the radar, then switch to the map and zoom out to see where the cloud ends.
