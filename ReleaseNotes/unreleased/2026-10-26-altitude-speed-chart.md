The logbook's Stats tab now opens on an altitude-vs-speed chart: every aircraft you've logged as a dot at its closest pass, coloured by airline, cargo, business jet, general aviation, helicopter or military. Tap a dot, or drag sideways along them, to read one. On Apple TV, select the chart to open it full screen, step from dot to dot with the remote and click for the full card; Back returns to Stats.
Try: open Logbook → Stats and drag a finger along the band of airliners cruising up top.
